# NISR 2026 Big Data Hackathon: verified brief

Source: [NISR 2026 Hackathon Competition](https://www.statistics.gov.rw/about/hackathon/2026-hackathon-competition), accessed 24 October 2026.

Track 2 is **Financial Inclusion & Poverty Reduction**. It asks for data-informed understanding of financial exclusion, poverty dynamics, and social-protection impact. The solution must use NISR data, optionally with open/public data related to poverty, household income, or financial access. Practical impact for vulnerable households, policymakers, or civil society is encouraged.

The published registration deadline was 20 October 2026 at 11:59 PM and the submission deadline is 30 October 2026 at 11:59 PM. As of the access date, registration is past and submission is upcoming. The page gives no timezone for these deadlines.

Participation is for undergraduate or master's students with eligibility proof. Each team must have two students and at least one Rwandan citizen. Submissions are expected to include a public GitHub link, deployed app link, and documentation. AI tools are allowed if disclosed; entrants are responsible for their work and plagiarism is prohibited. The page also excludes prior first-, second-, or third-place winners.

Evaluation assigns 20 points each to problem understanding/relevance, data use/methodology, technical innovation, usability/design, and tangible impact. The page says entrants agree to assign and transfer all IP rights in submitted work, including copyright and patent, to NISR, which reserves rights to use, reproduce, modify, publish, and distribute submitted work for any purpose.

The competition page is the verified source for these requirements. No separate webinar or video guidance was verified, and no separate IP contract was reviewed. Confirm any timezone or later amendments with NISR. This summary is not a legal interpretation.
