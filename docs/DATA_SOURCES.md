# Data sources

Sources below are official NISR pages or report files. Accessed 24 October 2026.

| Source | Use in this bundle | Locator |
|---|---|---|
| [FinScope Survey (2024), NISR](https://www.statistics.gov.rw/data-sources/surveys/FinScope-Survey) | 96% financially included adults; 2024 headline summary | Publication summary: 96% (7.8 million), versus 93% in 2020 |
| [Rwanda FinScope Survey 2024 report (PDF)](https://www.statistics.gov.rw/sites/default/files/documents/2024-09/Rwanda-Finscope-2024-Report_compressed.pdf) | 52% saving via group; 86% own/use mobile money | Figure 35, printed p. 45; section 3.2 and Figure 25, printed p. 40 |
| [EICV7 Main Indicators Report 2023/24](https://www.statistics.gov.rw/statistical-publications/poverty-and-social-protection/eicv7-main-indicators-report-202324) | 27.4% poverty estimate | Official publication summary headline |
| [EICV7 survey page](https://www.statistics.gov.rw/data-sources/surveys/EICV/integrated-household-living-conditions-survey-7-eicv-7) | Survey context, period, methodological change | Survey page; October 2023–October 2024 |

The NISR FinScope survey page also describes the survey period as 6 September–8 October 2024 and sampling as 14,000 households. The values here are published adult-level estimates; the sample count is not treated as an adult denominator. EICV7 used changes to poverty measurement methodology, so comparisons with earlier published poverty figures require care. No microdata was used.
